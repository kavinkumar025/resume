Minyfin

Help people who search for a specific money question land on a guide or calculator that answers it.

  • Published dedicated pages for budgeting, emergency funds, and saving from a first salary, alongside eight calculators with examples, formulas, and assumptions.
  • Gave guides and calculator pages their own descriptive titles and summaries. Marked the preferred address for each page so search engines can group duplicate addresses together.
  • Listed guides, tools, and article images in the sitemap, a list of pages for search engines. Added page details that help them recognize articles and understand where each guide sits on the site.

LIC Guidance in Erode

Help people in Erode district find relevant guidance before contacting an independent advisor.

  • Created individual pages for common searches such as claim assistance, LIC branch information, premium payments, and child and retirement planning.
  • Added clear page titles and summaries, plus site information that identifies the advisor, the location, and the area served.
  • Added preferred page addresses, guide images, sharing previews, and a sitemap covering service pages, articles, and their images.
  • Added a Search Console verification tag so the site owner can use Google’s dashboard to inspect pages and measure search activity.

GymTracker

Give people a useful answer when they search for a way to log workouts or understand their progress.

  • Published public articles on tracking attendance, logging a workout, reading weight trends, and using the 360-degree body model.
  • Gave articles individual titles, summaries, cover images, and preferred addresses. Added page details so search engines can recognize a blog article and identify its publisher.
  • Listed the blog and cover images in the sitemap, enabled large image previews, and added a Search Console verification tag.

AutoMax Used-Car Demo

Help people find the showroom and its guide while keeping fictional listings out of search results.

  • Moved the demo to one public address and redirected visitors from the original Vercel address.
  • Published an English and Tamil product guide with its own search title, summary, preferred address, and sharing preview.
  • Listed public pages such as the catalog and guide in a sitemap. The sample vehicle page sends a request to search engines to keep it out of their results.
